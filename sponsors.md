# Sponsor TABConf 8

**October 12-15, 2026 at the Georgia Tech Exhibition Hall in Atlanta.** This is the final TABConf, so this is the last chance to put your name on it.

TABConf is a technical conference. The room is protocol and application developers, the people actually building on Bitcoin, and the programming is curated for signal rather than hype. If those are the people you want to reach, sponsoring is the most direct way to do it.

> **For the most up to date info on sponsorship availability, please contact [hello@tabconf.com](mailto:hello@tabconf.com).**
> Packages sell out. This page lists what exists, not what is still open.

### Grant Sponsor
**$10,000 · Unlimited**

Grant money is used for one thing: bringing more people to TABConf who would not otherwise be able to come. It covers tickets, travel, and lodging.

Recipients are prioritized by their development work in the space, so the funding goes to people actively building on and contributing to Bitcoin rather than to whoever applies first. See the [grant program]({{ '/grants/' | relative_url }}) for details.

There is no cap on this one. Every additional grant sponsor means more builders in the room, so we will take as many as we can get.

- Named as a supporter of the TABConf Developer Grant Program
- Logo on the grants page and the sponsors page
- 4 sponsor tickets

## Not sure which one fits?

Tell us what you are trying to get out of the event, whether that is hiring developers, launching something, getting feedback on a product, or just supporting the community, and we will tell you honestly which package makes sense. Sometimes the answer is a smaller one than you expected.

We can also put together something that is not on this list. Several of the best sponsorships we have run started as an email asking whether an idea was possible.

**Email [hello@tabconf.com](mailto:hello@tabconf.com).**

## How it works

1. Email [hello@tabconf.com](mailto:hello@tabconf.com) with the package you want, or a description of what you are after.
2. We confirm availability and send an invoice. You can pay in **fiat via Stripe** or in **bitcoin via OpenNode**.
3. Send us a high resolution logo and we add you to this page.
4. Closer to the event we follow up about tickets, table setup, and anything your package needs on site.

> **Availability changes. For the most up to date info on sponsorship availability, please contact [hello@tabconf.com](mailto:hello@tabconf.com).**

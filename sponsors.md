# Sponsor TABConf 8

**October 12-15, 2026 at the Georgia Tech Exhibition Hall in Atlanta.** This is the final TABConf, so this is the last chance to put your name on it.

TABConf is a technical conference. The room is protocol and application developers, the people actually building on Bitcoin, and the programming is curated for signal rather than hype. If those are the people you want to reach, sponsoring is the most direct way to do it.

---

## Sponsorship is limited at this point

We are close to the event and most of it is spoken for. **If you are interested, email [hello@tabconf.com](mailto:hello@tabconf.com) and we will tell you honestly what is left.**

Two things are still open to anyone.

### General sponsorship

The straightforward way to back the conference. You get your logo on the [sponsors page]({{ '/our-sponsors/' | relative_url }}) and sponsor tickets to the event.

### Capture the Bitcoin donations

[Capture the Bitcoin]({{ '/ctb/' | relative_url }}) is the scavenger hunt challenge that runs through the conference every year. **Donations of any size fund the prizes and the challenges**, and if you would rather build a challenge around your own technology, we will help you do that.

---

## Something else in mind?

Ask. Several of the best sponsorships we have run started as an email asking whether an idea was possible, and we can usually make room for a good one.

Tell us what you are trying to get out of the event, whether that is hiring developers, launching something, getting feedback on a product, or just supporting the community.

**Email [hello@tabconf.com](mailto:hello@tabconf.com).**

---

## How it works

1. Email us with what you are after.
2. We confirm what is available and send an invoice. Pay in **fiat via Stripe** or in **bitcoin via OpenNode**.
3. Send us a high resolution logo and we add you to the sponsors page.
4. Closer to the event we follow up about tickets, table setup, and anything else you need on site.

# Schedule

TABConf’s schedule is **open source**: sessions and villages are organized in the open, and the live site pulls from our tooling.

- **Live schedule:** [schedule.tabconf.com](https://schedule.tabconf.com/)  
- **Source of truth on GitHub:** browse [TABConf org projects](https://github.com/orgs/TABConf/projects) for the active TABConf 8 board when it is published.

### Times

| | |
|-------|------|
| **Doors open** | Monday, Oct 12 at 11:00 AM |
| **Event ends** | Thursday, Oct 15 at 5:00 PM |
| **Closing ceremony** | Wednesday, Oct 14 at 4:00 PM |

The closing ceremony is on Wednesday rather than the final day, so that as many people as possible are still in the room for it. Thursday runs a full day afterwards.

### Format reminder

| Dates | What |
|-------|------|
| **Oct 12-13** | Builder Days and workshops, two rooms |
| **Oct 14-15** | Conference sessions, main stage |
